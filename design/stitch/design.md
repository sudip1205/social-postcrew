Postcrew Studio: a friendly control room for a crew of AI agents that write and publish social content.

Principles: icons beside every label (line icons, 24px, rounded), plain-language helper text under every setting, status shown by colour AND icon AND word (never colour alone), generous whitespace, soft cards with 12px corners.

Status colour code: Success/passed = green #059669 (check-circle). Running/working = violet #7C3AED (animated spinner). Waiting/awaiting approval = amber #D97706 (clock). Warning/expiring = orange #EA580C (alert-triangle). Error/failed/vetoed = red #E11D48 (x-circle). Info/practice mode = blue #0284C7 (info). Skipped/idle = slate #94A3B8.

App colour code: LinkedIn = blue #0A66C2 with in-badge, YouTube = red #FF0033 with play-badge. Each app's tab, cards and calendar entries carry its colour as a left border and badge.

Agent teams keep a gradient avatar: Command violet, Planning sky, Research teal, Writing orange, Design pink, Quality indigo, Operations slate.

Interactive text: key terms have a dotted underline with a hover tooltip explaining them; inline editable text shows a pencil icon on hover; chips are clickable filters; buttons have an icon and a verb (Approve, Veto, Run now).

Density: comfortable. Mobile-first responsive. Accessible contrast AA.
